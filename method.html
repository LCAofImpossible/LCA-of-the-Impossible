THE METHOD

From myth to model.
From model to footprint.

LCA of the Impossible does not try to make impossible subjects physically real. It makes them sufficiently defined to ask a real life-cycle question: what service is being delivered, what must exist for that service to happen, and where would the climate-change burden plausibly land?

How to read an episode ↓ See the seven-stage method →
Make the assumptions visible.

Uncertainty is part of the model. It is declared, structured and tested rather than hidden behind false precision.

Keep the audit trail alive.

A result should remain traceable back through activity data, modelling choices, proxies and source evidence.

Interpret, do not just calculate.

The purpose is to reveal hotspots, sensitivities and life-cycle logic — not merely to produce a large number.

HOW TO READ AN EPISODE

Start with the basis.
Then read the number.

A headline footprint is never self-explanatory. Read it as the output of a declared reporting basis, a reconstructed system and a defined boundary. The sequence below shows where to look and what each part can — and cannot — tell you.

FULL AUDIT ROUTE

Question → Model → Inventory → Factors → Result → Tests

Follow the complete calculation path, including included and excluded processes, cut-off choices, allocations, proxies and source context.

01

HEADLINE & REPORTING BASIS

Ask what one result represents.

Read the climate result together with the functional unit or reporting basis: the service, quantity, quality, delivery condition and timeframe. The reference flow states what must be supplied to deliver that unit.

READ TOGETHER

Result, unit, functional unit or reporting basis, reference flow and timeframe.

02

SUBJECT & RECONSTRUCTION

See which version became the model.

Check the chosen narrative version and the geometry, mass, materials, lifetime and operating pattern used to make it quantifiable. Separate sourced facts from reconstruction, inference and declared assumptions.

LOOK FOR

Dimensions, material choices, lifetime, use pattern and the evidence status behind them.

03

INVENTORY & SYSTEM BOUNDARY

Find what is counted — and what is not.

The inventory lists the quantified flows. Boundary notes identify included and excluded processes, the cut-off event and any lifetime allocation, reuse, recycling or avoided-production treatment.

CHECK CONSISTENCY

Every counted flow should have a quantity, unit, modelling basis and declared treatment.

04

EMISSION FACTORS & PROXIES

Trace each quantity to its climate intensity.

Check that factor units match activity units and that source, year, geography and technology are relevant. When an impossible process needs an analogue, the proxy and its rationale should remain visible.

How sources and factors are selected →Open the LCA glossary →

FOLLOW THE MATH

Activity data × emission factor = contribution. Contributions must reconcile to the total.

05

BASELINE, CONTRIBUTIONS & HOTSPOT

Read the total first, then its structure.

The baseline is the result for the declared scenario. Contribution shares show where that total comes from; the hotspot is the flow, stage or assumption that most strongly controls the result.

RECONCILE

Displayed contributions should add to the baseline within the stated rounding precision.

06

SENSITIVITY & INTERPRETATION

Test what changes the conclusion.

Compare the baseline with low and high cases that change one physical or data/model parameter at a time. Use the Evidence Profile to understand evidence confidence, proxy dependence and assumption sensitivity; then judge whether the final interpretation stays within those limits.

DO NOT READ AS A SCORE

Low, Medium and High are qualitative transparency indicators, not formal data-quality ratings or uncertainty distributions.

MODEL PASSPORT

A map of the case, not the whole territory.

The Passport is a quick orientation layer: it gathers the registered reporting basis, headline result, hotspot, Evidence Profile and uncertainty note. It does not replace the detailed inventory, boundary notes, factor ledger or source trail, and it is not a verification statement.

Before comparing two episodes: align the functional units or reporting bases, system boundaries, timeframes and geographic or technological contexts. A larger headline number may simply describe a larger service.

IMPOSSIBLE-TO-INVENTORY PIPELINE

Seven steps.
One traceable model.

Each case passes through the same analytical logic even when the subject changes completely. The process starts with a narrative object and ends with an interpreted footprint whose assumptions remain visible.

01

Define the subject

Establish what is actually being assessed and which version of the impossible subject belongs inside the model.

02

Establish the evidence

Separate direct evidence, engineering analogues, inference and explicit assumptions.

03

Define the functional unit

Turn the story into a quantified service, event, journey, lifetime or other reporting basis.

04

Reconstruct the system

Translate form and function into geometry, materials, energy, movement, operation and maintenance.

05

Build the inventory

Assign activity data, system boundaries, proxies, quantities and modelling bases to the reconstructed system.

06

Calculate the footprint

Connect activity data to the best available emission factors while preserving the calculation path.

07

Interpret the result

Identify hotspots, test sensitivities and explain what the number means within the limits of the scenario.

EVIDENCE LADDER

Not all data are born equal.

An impossible LCA can contain strong evidence and speculative reconstruction at the same time. The relevant question is not whether every input is certain, but whether its provenance and influence on the result are visible.

The ladder below describes provenance. It is not a score and does not imply that every episode must contain all four levels.

Known

Directly stated, measured, historically documented or otherwise supported by explicit source evidence.

Reconstructed

Derived from dimensions, mechanics, engineering relationships or defensible physical reconstruction.

Inferred

Not directly stated, but supported by contextual evidence, comparable systems or a reasoned analogue.

Assumed

A declared scenario choice required to close the model where the available evidence cannot.

EMISSION FACTOR HIERARCHY

Specific when possible.
Transparent when not.

The factor is part of the modelling decision, not a decorative citation. Preference is given to the most specific defensible factor available for the activity being modelled; where that is not possible, the loss of specificity is made explicit.

01 · Preferred

Specific

A factor closely aligned with the material, technology, geography, fuel or activity represented by the inventory flow.

02 · Fallback

Representative

A credible dataset that represents the same class of activity when a more specific factor is unavailable or unjustified.

03 · Declared

Proxy

An analogue used because no defensible direct dataset exists. Proxy dependence is treated as part of the evidence profile.

WHAT THE NUMBER MEANS

A result is conditional, not absolute.

The final number is meaningful only together with the functional unit, reconstruction and assumptions that produced it. The method therefore treats interpretation as part of the result rather than an afterthought.

01

Not a claim of physical reality.

The subject may be impossible. The analysis concerns the reconstructed scenario, not proof that the object or event could exist exactly as described.

02

Not false precision.

Precision should follow the quality of the inventory. Sensitivities and evidence indicators show where a headline result can move.

03

Still a real LCA question.

Functional units, inventories, boundaries, emission factors, hotspots and sensitivity analysis remain useful even when the subject begins in fiction or myth.

THE AUDIT TRAIL

Every result needs a path back.

The website deliberately leaves the modelling logic visible. Episode inventories, model notes, evidence profiles, hotspots and sensitivities are designed to preserve the chain between the impossible subject and the published conclusion.
