THE TERRITORY

Machines become systems.

Season I covers science-fiction and speculative vehicles, robots, machines, devices, infrastructure, habitats, artificial systems and megastructures.

The original cases are being rebuilt and republished one at a time with a more explicit analysis. The range identifies the season’s editorial territory; the live count below identifies what is currently published.

EDITORIAL GRAMMAR

How a machine becomes an LCA.

The story establishes the technology. The model establishes what must physically happen.

01 · CONFIGURE

Define the narrative technology.

Fix the machine, mission, setting and constraints before deciding what the model needs to represent.

02 · DEFINE THE SERVICE

Put function before spectacle.

Translate the story into a functional unit: one mission, one delivered service, one operating period or one commissioned system.

03 · TRACE THE SYSTEM

Follow the machine in motion.

Reconstruct materials, manufacture, energy, infrastructure, maintenance and end-of-life wherever the chosen boundary requires them.

READ THE MODEL, NOT ONLY THE NUMBER

Results answer different questions.

A mission footprint, a construction footprint and a lifetime footprint are not directly comparable. Read each result together with its functional unit, boundary, hotspot and sensitivity.
