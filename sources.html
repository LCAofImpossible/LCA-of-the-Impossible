SOURCES & DATA

Every number needs
a provenance.

Impossible subjects do not come with product declarations or complete bills of materials. The model therefore depends on a controlled hierarchy of evidence, engineering reconstruction, recognized datasets and explicit proxies. This page explains how those layers are selected and kept visible.

SOURCE HIERARCHY

Evidence first.
Proxy last.

No single database is treated as a universal source of truth. Each episode uses the most specific defensible information available for the question being modelled, while the approved episode remains the authority for its own published assumptions and factors.

Direct evidence

Explicit narrative, historical, physical, technical or otherwise authoritative information that directly constrains the model.

Engineering reconstruction

Dimensions, mechanics, material requirements or process logic derived from defensible physical relationships and comparable systems.

Representative data

Recognized emission-factor datasets, technical literature or representative processes aligned as closely as possible with the inventory flow.

Declared proxy

A substitute dataset or analogue used only when a more specific basis is unavailable. The loss of specificity remains visible in the interpretation.

EMISSION FACTORS

Specific → Representative → Proxy.

The factor is part of the model. Selection therefore considers what activity is represented, how closely the dataset matches it and which components of the factor are appropriate for the declared boundary.

Specific

Closest defensible match

Preferred when a factor meaningfully aligns with the actual material, fuel, process, geography or transport activity represented by the inventory.

Representative

Controlled fallback

Used when a directly specific dataset is unavailable but a recognized process or category provides a reasonable representation of the modelled flow.

Proxy

Visible approximation

Used when neither a specific nor adequately representative dataset exists. Proxy dependence is then part of the episode's evidence profile and interpretation.

UK Government GHG Conversion Factors. Where DEFRA / UK Government factors are used, the model preserves distinctions that matter to the boundary — such as direct emissions, well-to-tank (WTT) components, transmission and distribution (T&D), outside-of-scopes reporting and biogenic treatment where applicable. The presence of a DEFRA factor does not automatically make it representative of every fictional system.

DATA VERSIONING

The dataset year matters.

New analyses may use newer factor sets, but a published episode is not silently recalculated every time a database changes. Revisions must be explicit so the published result and its audit trail remain reproducible.

Dataset identity

Name the source family or modelling basis sufficiently clearly for the factor choice to be understood.

Version / year

Retain the relevant release year or version when it materially affects reproducibility or interpretation.

Geography

Do not imply geographic representativeness that the underlying factor does not support.

Technology

Preserve meaningful distinctions between technologies, fuels, material routes or transport modes when the source allows them.

Factor components

Keep direct, upstream and separately reported components distinct when the chosen dataset separates them.

Proxy status

If the factor is standing in for an impossible or unknown process, disclose that modelling role rather than presenting the number as measured reality.

TRACEABILITY

Sources are part of the model.

The website does not manufacture a source list merely to make an episode look complete. Episode-specific evidence and factor choices must come from the approved analysis. Where source detail is unavailable, the limitation remains a limitation.

What is retained

Enough to follow the logic

Major activity data, modelling bases, proxies, exclusions, factor families and assumptions should remain recognizable from the published material and episode page.

What is not claimed

No artificial completeness

A neat bibliography does not make uncertain evidence certain. Missing information is not silently replaced with a generic source unless the modelling choice is explicitly declared.
