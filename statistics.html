INTERPRETATION GUARDRAIL

What is deliberately not calculated.

NO SUMMED FOOTPRINT

Different reporting bases cannot be added into a meaningful “total impact of the impossible”.

NO AVERAGE RESULT

A mean across missions, structures, lifetimes and repeated acts would have no coherent functional meaning.

NO PERFORMANCE RANKING

Magnitude is not better-or-worse performance when functional units, boundaries and assumptions differ.
