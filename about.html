ABOUT THE PROJECT

Life-cycle thinking,
where datasets stop.

LCA of the Impossible is an independent editorial and technical experiment: take a myth, legend, fictional machine or impossible structure, reconstruct it as a transparent system and use the logic of Life Cycle Assessment to ask a serious environmental question.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Make LCA visible and memorable.

Life Cycle Assessment is powerful precisely because it forces a question to become explicit: what service is being delivered, what is inside the system, which flows are required and where does the burden occur? Impossible subjects make those choices unusually visible.

The fantasy is the entry point. The real subject is the method.

01 · Visibility

Show the model.

Functional units, system boundaries, assumptions, proxies and hotspots are not backstage details. They are part of the story.

02 · Discipline

Respect uncertainty.

The impossible does not justify arbitrary numbers. Where evidence weakens, the reconstruction and its limitations become more explicit.

03 · Memory

Use narrative deliberately.

A memorable subject can carry technical meaning further than a generic example, provided storytelling never replaces the analytical logic.

WHAT IT IS

A technical editorial series.

Each episode is built around a quantified reporting basis, plausible reconstruction, life-cycle inventory, emission-factor selection, hotspot analysis and interpretation. The website preserves the central audit trail and lets the archive be explored by narrative category and LCA lens.

Analysis

Real LCA logic

The subjects may be impossible; the methodological questions are not. Functional units, inventories, allocation of flows, boundaries, proxies and sensitivities remain meaningful analytical tools.

Reconstruction

Plausibility over certainty

Engineering analogues and declared assumptions are used to make the subject modelable without pretending that speculative inputs are measured data.

Interpretation

The number is not the endpoint

The series focuses on why the result behaves as it does: which material, operation, journey, lifetime or repeated event controls the footprint.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Impossible does not mean unqualified.

The format is intentionally playful, but the limits are explicit. These cases are analytical reconstructions, not verified environmental declarations for physically existing products.

Not a verification claim

No fictional product is “certified”.

Evidence Profile indicators are editorial transparency tools. They are not formal ISO data-quality ratings, assurance conclusions or third-party verification statements.

Not a comparative claim

Different functional units remain different.

Headline results across episodes cannot be treated as like-for-like environmental rankings unless the systems, functions and boundaries are genuinely comparable.

BEHIND THE SERIES

Independent by design.

LCA of the Impossible is created by Alberto, an auditor and consultant working with Life Cycle Assessment, product carbon footprints and environmental verification. The project is separate from client work and uses impossible systems as a public laboratory for explaining modelling choices, data limitations and life-cycle interpretation.

The objective is not to make LCA less rigorous. It is to make the reasoning behind rigorous LCA easier to see.
