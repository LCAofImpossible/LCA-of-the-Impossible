Impossible stories, reconstructed as traceable systems.

THE TERRITORY

Stories acquire inventories.

Season II covers myths, legends, folklore, legendary beings, places, objects, rites, punishments and supernatural systems.

The reconstruction never claims that the impossible is real. It asks what materials, energy, transport and repeated activity would be required by a declared physical analogue.

EDITORIAL GRAMMAR

How a story becomes an LCA.

Narrative evidence establishes the rules. The model makes their physical consequences explicit.

01 · CONSTRAIN

Identify what the story demands.

Extract the recurring act, setting, scale and narrative constraints that the reconstruction must preserve.

02 · ANALOGUE

Give the impossible a physical proxy.

Choose an explicit engineering analogue for the being, place, object, rite or supernatural mechanism under study.

03 · TRACE

Turn consequence into a system.

Quantify the resulting materials, energy, movement, repetition and lifetime effects without hiding proxy dependence.

READ THE MODEL, NOT ONLY THE NUMBER

Results answer different questions.

A single act, a yearly repetition and a constructed world do not share a reporting basis. Read each result together with its functional unit, boundary, hotspot and sensitivity.
