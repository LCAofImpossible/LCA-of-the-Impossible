EPISODE #35

The Flying Dutchman

What is the carbon footprint of a ship condemned to sail forever?

528.2 t CO₂efor one year of cursed navigation, including full construction

THE SUBJECT

A ship that never reaches harbour.

Reconstructed as a large early-17th-century East Indiaman, using historical ship analogues to estimate hull mass, rigging, armament, construction and operation.

01

Functional unit

One year of navigation, with the entire construction burden attributed to the functional unit.

02

Main hotspot

Construction — approximately 97.4% of the main result.

03

Method

Engineering reconstruction + transparent emission factors.

THE VERDICT

The ship may never reach harbour. Its footprint arrived before it ever left. The sea forgets every wake. Carbon does not.
