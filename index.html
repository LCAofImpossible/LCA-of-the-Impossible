THE METHOD

Fantasy in.
Inventory out.

Each episode starts with an impossible subject and treats it as an engineering problem: define a functional unit, reconstruct a plausible inventory, select transparent emission factors and calculate the carbon footprint.

The result is not a verified ISO study. It is a narrative and engineering exercise designed to make LCA methodology visible, understandable and memorable.
