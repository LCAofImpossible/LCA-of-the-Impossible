THE METHOD

Fantasy in.
Inventory out.

Every episode turns an impossible subject into a traceable LCA question: define what service is being assessed, reconstruct a plausible system, quantify the inventory and interpret where the footprint lands.

The goal is not to simulate certainty where none exists. Evidence, proxies and assumptions remain visible so the result can be read together with the model that produced it.

Explore the full methodology →
01 · Define

Turn the story into a question.

Choose the subject, reporting basis, functional unit and system boundary.

02 · Reconstruct

Make the impossible explicit.

Use source evidence, analogues, engineering logic and declared assumptions.

03 · Quantify

Build the inventory.

Translate the reconstruction into activity data, proxies and emission factors.

04 · Interpret

Reveal what drives the result.

Read hotspots, sensitivities and uncertainty together with the headline footprint.
